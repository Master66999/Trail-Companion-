Autonomous On-Device Vision

Powered by open-weight CLIP ViT-B/16 quantized to int8 via Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime Web. Your camera photos never leave your device. Download the weights once on Wi-Fi (~87 MB), and identify flora and fauna deep in the backcountry with zero cell towers.

Screen-Free Walk Meter

We don't want you glued to your glass screen. Our Walk Mode measures how much of your outdoor time was spent off-screen, encouraging you to pocket the phone and observe nature directly.

Point your camera at a laptop or indoor desk, and the model's zero-shot negative bank flags it immediately, reminding you to close the laptop and step into the sunlight.
